About

I am a pharmacist with an MMSc in Health Economics, Policy and Management from Karolinska Institutet, where I studied on a Swedish Institute Scholarship. My master's thesis, submitted in May 2026, is the first independent head-to-head cost-utility analysis of treatment strategies in metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer from a Swedish healthcare perspective, using patient-level data from the ProBio platform trial and conducted in adherence to TLV methodological guidelines.

Before moving to Sweden, I worked for four years across more than ten health systems projects in the Philippines and the Asia Pacific region: national benefit package design, WHO-supported oncology priority-setting, pharmaceutical patient access programme operations, and applied economic evaluation. I have contributed to data collection, analysis, and drafting on two peer-reviewed publications in the Journal of Medical Economics.

My work consistently sits at the intersection of methods and policy: constructing the evidence that informs what gets funded, how much it costs, and who gets access. I am based in Stockholm.
