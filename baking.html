Baking

Baking has been a quiet constant throughout most of my adult life — something I return to regardless of what else is going on. I bake mostly bread, pastries, and the occasional layer cake, usually with whatever is in season or whatever I am curious about that week.

Living in Stockholm has expanded what I reach for: rye, cardamom, dark syrups, and the particular restraint of Scandinavian baking sit alongside the flavours I grew up with in the Philippines. The two do not always mix, but it is interesting to try.

I document what I make on Instagram — more for memory than performance.
