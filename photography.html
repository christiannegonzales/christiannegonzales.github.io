Photography

Amateur digital photography — no formal training, just curiosity and a camera I have been learning to use slowly over the past few years.

I shoot mostly street scenes, light, and the ordinary details of places I pass through. Moving between the Philippines and Sweden has sharpened what I notice: the quality of northern light in winter, the density of a Manila street mid-afternoon, the way the same hour feels completely different depending on where you are.

I keep a running archive on Instagram, mostly unedited or lightly processed.
