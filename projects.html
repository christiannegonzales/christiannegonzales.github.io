Economic Evaluation & HTA

Cost-Utility Analysis of Treatment Strategies in Metastatic Castration-Resistant Prostate Cancer in Sweden Master's Thesis · Karolinska Institutet · Submitted May 2026
▾
Partitioned Survival Model R / hesim Swedish HTA Oncology TLV Framework

The first independent head-to-head cost-utility analysis of treatment strategies in first-line mCRPC from a Swedish healthcare perspective, using patient-level data from a randomised platform trial. The analysis compared androgen receptor pathway inhibitors against taxanes and physician's choice, using a three-state partitioned survival model built in R and conducted in adherence to TLV methodological guidelines.

Economic Burden and Cost-Effectiveness of CKD Management in the Philippines Published Research · Journal of Medical Economics · 2025–2026
▾
Cost-of-Illness Cost-Effectiveness Peer-Reviewed Insurance Coverage

Two peer-reviewed studies examining the economic dimensions of chronic kidney disease in the Philippine health system. The cost-of-illness study quantified direct and indirect costs across disease stages using insurance claims analysis. The cost-effectiveness study evaluated empagliflozin for CKD, adapting a global model to the Philippine payer context and generating an ICER relevant to PhilHealth reimbursement decisions.

Health Policy & Coverage Design

Benefit Package Design for Acute Cardiac Care: Costing Analysis for PhilHealth STEMI Coverage Applied Policy Work · Alliance for Improving Health Outcomes · 2020–2021
▾
Benefit Package Design Claims Data Unit Costing National Insurance

Constructing cost evidence to support the design of a bundled, episode-based reimbursement package for ST-elevation myocardial infarction within the Philippine national insurance system. The work involved building unit cost datasets from insurance claims and provider data and coordinating provider network consultations across three regions.

Expanding Insurance Coverage for Childhood Cancers: Evidence Synthesis for WHO-WPRO Priority-Setting Evidence Synthesis · WHO Western Pacific Regional Office · 2022
▾
Evidence Synthesis Priority-Setting Paediatric Oncology UHC

Supporting the WHO Western Pacific Regional Office in expanding national insurance coverage for childhood cancers. The work synthesised clinical practice guidelines, drafted standards of care for selected paediatric cancer types, and coordinated validation meetings with professional medical societies, framing eligibility thresholds around both clinical priority and system-level feasibility.
