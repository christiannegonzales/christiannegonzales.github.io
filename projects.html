Cost-Utility Analysis of Treatment Strategies in Metastatic Castration-Resistant Prostate Cancer in Sweden Master's Thesis · Karolinska Institutet · Submitted May 2026
▾
Partitioned Survival Model R / hesim Swedish HTA Oncology TLV Framework

A cost-utility analysis of treatment strategies in first-line mCRPC from a Swedish healthcare perspective, using patient-level data from a randomised platform trial. The analysis compared androgen receptor pathway inhibitors against taxanes and physician's choice, using a three-state partitioned survival model built in R and conducted in adherence to TLV methodological guidelines. To the best of my knowledge, this is among the first analyses of this kind conducted using patient-level trial data in the Swedish reimbursement context.

HTA & Market Access

Real-World Evidence in ATMP HTA Submissions: A Cross-HTA Landscape Analysis for JCA Readiness Independent project · July 2026–present · Ongoing
▾
Real-World Evidence ATMPs Joint Clinical Assessment HTA AI-Assisted Methods Prompt Engineering TLV G-BA

The EU HTA Regulation introduced Joint Clinical Assessments (JCA) as a mandatory EU-level review of new medicines, with Advanced Therapy Medicinal Products (ATMPs) among the first categories in scope. ATMPs present a distinctive evidentiary challenge: small populations, single arm trials, and no active comparator mean real world evidence is increasingly expected to fill submission gaps, but what national HTA bodies have actually accepted has not been systematically mapped.

This project, pursued independently out of interest in the intersection of RWE, HTA methods, and EU market access, examines publicly available ATMP assessment documents from TLV (Sweden) and G-BA (Germany) to identify where they diverge and what JCA will require. The approach combines a structured RWE classification framework with prompt engineering, extracting dual layer outputs that retain original language passages alongside AI generated classifications for auditability. Findings are mapped against JCA PICO requirements to produce a prospective framework guiding RWE strategy for ATMPs entering the JCA pipeline.
