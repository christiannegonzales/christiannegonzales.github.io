Master's Thesis · Karolinska Institutet · Submitted May 2026

Cost-Utility Analysis of Treatment Strategies in Metastatic Castration-Resistant Prostate Cancer in Sweden

Partitioned Survival Model R / hesim Swedish HTA Oncology TLV Framework

The first independent head-to-head cost-utility analysis of treatment strategies in first-line mCRPC from a Swedish healthcare perspective, using patient-level data from the a randomised platform trial. The analysis compared androgen receptor pathway inhibitors against taxanes and physician's choice, using a three-state partitioned survival model built in R and conducted in adherence to TLV methodological guidelines.

Published Research · Journal of Medical Economics · 2025–2026

Economic Burden and Cost-Effectiveness of CKD Management in the Philippines

Cost-of-Illness Cost-Effectiveness Peer-Reviewed Insurance Coverage

Two peer-reviewed studies examining the economic dimensions of chronic kidney disease in the Philippine health system. The cost-of-illness study quantified direct and indirect costs across disease stages using insurance claims analysis. The cost-effectiveness study evaluated empagliflozin for CKD, adapting a global model to the Philippine payer context and generating an ICER relevant to PhilHealth reimbursement decisions.

Benefit Package Design Claims Data Unit Costing National Insurance

Constructing cost evidence to support the design of a bundled, episode-based reimbursement package for ST-elevation myocardial infarction within the Philippine national insurance system.
