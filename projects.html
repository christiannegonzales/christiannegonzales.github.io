Real-World Evidence in ATMP HTA Submissions: A Cross-HTA Landscape Analysis for JCA Readiness Independent project · July 2026–present · Ongoing
▾
Real-World Evidence ATMPs Joint Clinical Assessment HTA AI-Assisted Methods Prompt Engineering TLV G-BA

The EU HTA Regulation mandates Joint Clinical Assessments (JCA) for new medicines, with ATMPs among the first categories in scope. ATMPs face a distinct evidentiary challenge: small trial populations, single-arm designs, and absent comparators mean real-world evidence is increasingly expected to fill submission gaps — yet what national HTA bodies have accepted in practice remains unmapped.

This independent project examines ATMP assessment documents from TLV (Sweden) and G-BA (Germany) to identify cross-HTA divergences and define what JCA will demand. Using a structured RWE classification framework combined with prompt engineering, it extracts dual-layer outputs that preserve original language alongside AI-generated classifications for auditability. Findings are mapped against JCA PICO requirements to produce a prospective framework for RWE strategy for ATMPs entering the JCA pipeline.
